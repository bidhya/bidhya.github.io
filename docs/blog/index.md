# Blog

Dated, opinionated writing: field reports from building and running things, and notes on
tooling that will look different in a year.

This is deliberately separate from the [Engineering Notes](../notes/index.md). Those are
evergreen references, maintained in place as understanding improves. Posts here are
snapshots — they carry a date and a version stamp because they describe a moving target,
and they are left standing rather than quietly updated.

Posts are filed under the same topic vocabulary as the rest of the site.
